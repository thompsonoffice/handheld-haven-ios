ENTRY: MSI Claw A1M
Maker: MSI
Released: March 8 and 15, 2024 (Wikipedia).
Launch price: US$699.99 (Core Ultra 5 135H, 512 GB), US$749.99 (Core Ultra 7 155H, 512 GB), US$799.99 (Core Ultra 7, 1 TB) (Wikipedia).

WHAT IT CAN DO
MSI's first handheld and the first major one with Intel. Reviewers criticized high power draw and lower performance than rivals; Wired called battery life poor (Wikipedia).

HARDWARE
Processor: Intel Core Ultra 5 135H (14 cores/18 threads, up to 4.6 GHz) or Core Ultra 7 155H (16 cores/22 threads, up to 4.8 GHz), Meteor Lake (Wikipedia).
Memory: 16 GB LPDDR5-6400; soldered.
Graphics: Intel Arc (Alchemist-based) with 8 Xe cores, up to 2.2 to 2.25 GHz.
Storage: 512 GB or 1 TB NVMe PCIe 4.0 M.2 2230; UHS-II microSD.
Display: 7-inch 1920 by 1080 IPS, 120 Hz, 48 to 120 Hz VRR, 500 nits.
Battery: 53 Wh.
Weight and size: 675 grams; 294 by 117 by 21.2 millimetres (VideoCardz).
Operating system: Windows 11 Home or Pro.
Wi-Fi and network: Wi-Fi 7, Killer BE1750 (VideoCardz).
Bluetooth: Bluetooth 5.4.
Cellular: None.
Ports and expansion (USB4, Thunderbolt, eGPU, video out, microSD, docking): One Thunderbolt 4 USB-C (DisplayPort, PD 3.0), microSD, audio jack. Thunderbolt 4 allows eGPU docks in principle.
Other hardware (fingerprint or Windows Hello, cameras, speakers, headphone jack, haptics, gyro): 2 by 2 W stereo speakers, hi-res audio ready; RGB hall-effect sticks; black outer coating.

CONTROLS AND BUTTON LAYOUT
RGB A/B/X/Y, RGB hall-effect sticks, standard handheld layout.

HOW IT FEELS
About 1.5 pounds with a black coating; the Claw name refers to its deep grips (grip depth not measured in sources).

BLIND USABILITY
No keyboard is built in, so the standard Narrator shortcut (Windows key+Ctrl+Enter) needs a USB or Bluetooth keyboard. Microsoft's alternative for touch devices, Windows button+Volume Up, needs a hardware Windows button, which this device does not list in its controls, so it should not be relied on (UNCERTAIN whether the maker maps a combination to it). Can I Play That's review of the ROG Xbox Ally X found Narrator is available right away during Windows setup, but setup only mentions keyboard combinations, not controller buttons, and blind users may struggle to turn it on with the small on-screen icons. The same applies to any Windows 11 handheld. Plan to plug in a keyboard for first setup. Once Windows is set up, turn on Start Narrator before sign-in (or install NVDA), and remap a spare back button to a keyboard shortcut if the maker's software allows it. MSI Center M accessibility is not documented by any reviewer found; one AppleVis commenter said it was "not that accessible last time I heard" (second-hand, UNCERTAIN).

GAME COMPATIBILITY
Performance tier: mid, below the Z1 Extreme rivals at launch (Wikipedia; The Verge review cited in later coverage). Intel Arc driver compatibility with older games was a known concern at launch (general reporting, not detailed here, UNCERTAIN).

UNCERTAIN
Arc older-game issues UNCERTAIN.

ENTRY: MSI Claw 7 AI+

Released: Pre-orders December 3, 2024; release around December 25, 2024 to January 2025 (The Verge; Windows Central).

Graphics: Intel Arc 140V (integrated).
Storage: M.2 2230 PCIe Gen4 SSD; microSD.
Display: 7-inch 1920 by 1080 IPS-level, 120 Hz, VRR, 500 nits, 100 percent sRGB.
Battery: 54.5 Wh; 65 W USB-C PD.
Weight and size: 675 grams; 290 by 117 by 21.2 millimetres.
Operating system: Windows 11 Home.
Wi-Fi and network: Intel Wi-Fi 6E AX211 (VideoCardz).
Bluetooth: Bluetooth 5.3.
Cellular: None.
Ports and expansion (USB4, Thunderbolt, eGPU, video out, microSD, docking): Two Thunderbolt 4 USB-C (DisplayPort, PD 3.0), microSD, audio combo jack.
Other hardware (fingerprint or Windows Hello, cameras, speakers, headphone jack, haptics, gyro): Power button with fingerprint sensor; redesigned hall-effect sticks and triggers, updated bumpers and D-pad.

Memory: 32 GB LPDDR5x-8533 on-package.

Storage: 1 TB M.2 2230 PCIe Gen4 SSD with an easy-access slot; microSD.
Display: 8-inch 1920 by 1200, 16:10, 120 Hz, VRR, 500 nits.
Battery: 80 Wh.
Weight and size: 795 grams (MSI); Notebookcheck measured 793 grams; 299 by 126 by 24 millimetres.
Operating system: Windows 11 Home.
Wi-Fi and network: Intel Wi-Fi 7 BE201 (VideoCardz).
Bluetooth: Bluetooth 5.4.
Cellular: None.

Other hardware (fingerprint or Windows Hello, cameras, speakers, headphone jack, haptics, gyro): Fingerprint power button. Notebookcheck: fans quieter than before but fluctuate under constant load; MSI software sometimes laggy.

ENTRY: MSI Claw A8
Maker: MSI
Released: Released between July 2025 and January 2026 depending on region (VideoCardz).
Launch price: EUR 999 recommended (Notebookcheck); US$1,149 on MSI's US store and Newegg (VideoCardz). Notebookcheck's price widget showed US$1,074.99 on Amazon as of November 14, 2026.

MSI's first AMD Claw. Notebookcheck (83 percent) found it offers no real added value over the Intel Claw 8, with a less ergonomic new case and smaller shoulder buttons and triggers.

HARDWARE
Processor: AMD Ryzen Z2 Extreme: 8 cores/16 threads (3 Zen 5 up to 5.0 GHz plus 5 Zen 5c up to 3.3 GHz); 20 W sustained, 50 W burst (Notebookcheck).
Memory: 24 GB LPDDR5X-8000; soldered.
Graphics: AMD Radeon 890M.
Storage: 1 TB M.2 2280 PCIe Gen4 SSD; microSD.
Display: 8-inch 1920 by 1200, 120 Hz, VRR.
Battery: 80 Wh (4-cell).
Weight and size: 765 grams; 299.5 by 126.2 by 24 millimetres.
Operating system: Windows 11 Home.
Wi-Fi and network: Wi-Fi 7 (chip not documented).
Bluetooth: Bluetooth 5.4.
Cellular: None.
Ports and expansion (USB4, Thunderbolt, eGPU, video out, microSD, docking): Two USB-C (USB4, DisplayPort, PD 3.0, Thunderbolt 4 compatible), microSD.
Other hardware (fingerprint or Windows Hello, cameras, speakers, headphone jack, haptics, gyro): Stereo speakers; 65 W adapter.

CONTROLS AND BUTTON LAYOUT
