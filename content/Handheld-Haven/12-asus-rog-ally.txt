ENTRY: ASUS ROG Ally (Z1 and Z1 Extreme)
Maker: ASUS, Republic of Gamers (ROG) brand
Released: Z1 Extreme model June 13, 2023; Z1 model September 18, 2023 (Wikipedia).
Launch price: US$699.99 (Z1 Extreme) and US$599.99 (Z1) (Wikipedia).

WHAT IT CAN DO
A Steam Deck-style Windows 11 handheld with a 7-inch 120 Hz 1080p screen, playing modern PC games from any store (Steam, Xbox, Epic and so on), plus full desktop Windows when docked.

HARDWARE
Processor: AMD Ryzen Z1 (Zen 4, 6 cores/12 threads, up to 4.9 GHz) or Ryzen Z1 Extreme (Zen 4, 8 cores/16 threads, up to 5.1 GHz), 4 nm (Wikipedia). TDP modes: not documented in sources used.
Memory: 16 GB LPDDR5-6400, quad-channel 32-bit (128-bit total), 102.4 GB/s; soldered.
Graphics: AMD RDNA 3 integrated: 4 compute units at up to 2.5 GHz (Z1) or 12 compute units at up to 2.7 GHz (Z1 Extreme).
Storage: 512 GB NVMe M.2 2230 SSD (replaceable) plus UHS-II microSD. ASUS acknowledged microSD cards and readers overheating and failing because the reader sits above the cooling system, and extended the warranty (Wikipedia).
Display: 7-inch 1920 by 1080 IPS touchscreen, 120 Hz, 500 nits, FreeSync Premium VRR, Dolby Vision.
Battery: not documented
Weight and size: 608 grams (Wikipedia).
Operating system: Windows 11.
Wi-Fi and network: not documented
Bluetooth: not documented
Cellular: None.
Ports and expansion (USB4, Thunderbolt, eGPU, video out, microSD, docking): ASUS proprietary-plus-USB-C port for the XG Mobile external GPU (commonly reported; not confirmed in sources used, UNCERTAIN), microSD UHS-II, headphone jack (not confirmed in sources used).
Other hardware (fingerprint or Windows Hello, cameras, speakers, headphone jack, haptics, gyro): Stereo speakers with Dolby Atmos. Power button with fingerprint scanner. Haptics and gyro: not documented in sources used.

CONTROLS AND BUTTON LAYOUT
Two analog sticks, LB and RB bumpers, LT and RT analog triggers, two rear grip buttons (M1, M2), D-pad, A/B/X/Y, View and Menu, a Command Center button, an Armoury Crate button, volume up and down, power with fingerprint (Wikipedia).

HOW IT FEELS
A flat, wide slab roughly 11 inches across (Tom's Hardware gives the Ally X as 11.02 by 4.37 by 1.45 inches, and the original shares its basic shape) with gently rounded grips, weighing 608 grams, about 1.3 pounds. The left side has the stick above the D-pad; the right side has the face buttons above the right stick, like an Xbox controller split in half around the screen. Two small paddles on the back fall under your middle fingers.

BLIND USABILITY
No keyboard is built in, so the standard Narrator shortcut (Windows key+Ctrl+Enter) needs a USB or Bluetooth keyboard. Microsoft's alternative for touch devices, Windows button+Volume Up, needs a hardware Windows button, which this device does not list in its controls, so it should not be relied on (UNCERTAIN whether the maker maps a combination to it). Can I Play That's review of the ROG Xbox Ally X found Narrator is available right away during Windows setup, but setup only mentions keyboard combinations, not controller buttons, and blind users may struggle to turn it on with the small on-screen icons. The same applies to any Windows 11 handheld. Plan to plug in a keyboard for first setup. Once Windows is set up, turn on Start Narrator before sign-in (or install NVDA), and remap a spare back button to a keyboard shortcut if the maker's software allows it. If Xbox mode (Microsoft's console-style full screen experience) is set to start at sign-in, Microsoft says other apps start only the first time you switch to the desktop. Set NVDA to Start at log in under Settings, Apps, Startup, or it may not be running in Xbox mode (Microsoft Support, KB5070297). Win+F11 enters and leaves Xbox mode. Armoury Crate SE: an AppleVis user with five months on an ROG Xbox Ally X said the Xbox full screen experience is fine to navigate with NVDA or Narrator, though not the best interface, and that Armoury Crate is "surprisingly accessible even down to remapping the embedded controller"; another AppleVis user returned theirs and found it frustrating, preferring Steam Big Picture. A blind Reddit user planning an Ally said he would connect a keyboard for initial setup. Can I Play That reports Armoury Crate can remap every button except the Xbox, Command Center and Library buttons, with macros of up to five key presses on one input, so a back button can be set to Windows+Ctrl+Enter style combinations where the macro allows (combination support per key not confirmed, UNCERTAIN).

GAME COMPATIBILITY
Performance tier: upper-mid for its year. Runs most current games at 720p to 1080p with reduced settings. Kernel anti-cheat games generally run because it is real Windows, though individual titles can block handhelds (not documented per device). All Windows audio games, NVDA-accessible indies and accessibility mods (for example Pokemon accessibility scripts mentioned by a blind Ally owner) run as on a desktop. The Z1 (non-Extreme) has only 4 GPU compute units, a third of the Extreme's, and is noticeably weaker.

UNCERTAIN
Ports and battery not confirmed in sources fetched for this book.

ENTRY: ASUS ROG Ally X
Maker: ASUS (ROG)
Released: Announced at Computex 2024; released July 22, 2024 (Wikipedia).
Launch price: US$799.99 (Wikipedia).

WHAT IT CAN DO
A high-end refresh of the Z1 Extreme Ally with more memory, bigger storage and a larger battery.

HARDWARE
Processor: AMD Ryzen Z1 Extreme (Zen 4, 8 cores/16 threads, up to 5.1 GHz).
Memory: 24 GB LPDDR5X-7500, 120 GB/s; soldered.
Graphics: AMD RDNA 3, 12 compute units, up to 2.7 GHz.
Storage: 1 TB NVMe M.2 2280 (full-length slot, easier to upgrade than the Ally's 2230) plus UHS-II microSD.
Display: 7-inch 1920 by 1080 IPS, 120 Hz, VRR.
Battery: Larger than the Ally (Wikipedia); 80 Wh per The Verge's Ayaneo 3 comparison.
Weight and size: 678 grams (Wikipedia); Tom's Hardware: 1.49 pounds, 11.02 by 4.37 by 1.45 inches.
Operating system: Windows 11.
Wi-Fi and network: not documented
Bluetooth: not documented
Cellular: None.
Ports and expansion (USB4, Thunderbolt, eGPU, video out, microSD, docking): not documented
Other hardware (fingerprint or Windows Hello, cameras, speakers, headphone jack, haptics, gyro): Fingerprint power button (as Ally). Other: not documented.

CONTROLS AND BUTTON LAYOUT
As the ROG Ally.

HOW IT FEELS
Similar to the Ally but a little thicker and heavier, about 1.5 pounds. Can I Play That's Josh, cited in their Xbox Ally X review, found the grey button legends hard to read, which matters for low vision users.

ENTRY: ROG Xbox Ally
Also known as: Codename Project Kennan
Maker: ASUS with Microsoft (ROG and Xbox brands)
Released: Unveiled June 8, 2025; pre-orders September 25, 2025; released October 16, 2025 (Wikipedia).
Launch price: US$599.99; EUR 599 (Wikipedia; Can I Play That).

WHAT IT CAN DO
The first Xbox-branded handheld PC. Boots into Xbox mode, a console-like launcher that lists games from Xbox, Steam and other stores, with the full Windows desktop a swipe or Win+F11 away.

HARDWARE
Processor: AMD Ryzen Z2 A: quad-core Zen 2 (Wikipedia).
Memory: 16 GB LPDDR5; soldered.
Graphics: AMD RDNA 2, 8 compute units at 1.6 GHz, about 1.6 TFLOPS.
Storage: 512 GB M.2 SSD (replaceable per Can I Play That), microSD.
Display: 7-inch IPS 1920 by 1080, 500 nits, 120 Hz, VRR. OLED was considered and rejected because of power draw with VRR.
Battery: 60 Wh (Can I Play That).
Weight and size: 670 grams; 290.8 by 121.5 by 50.7 millimetres.
Operating system: Windows 11 Home with Xbox mode.
Wi-Fi and network: Wi-Fi 6E 2x2 (chip not documented).
Bluetooth: Bluetooth 5.4.
Cellular: None.
Ports and expansion (USB4, Thunderbolt, eGPU, video out, microSD, docking): Two USB-C on the top edge, microSD reader, 3.5 mm audio jack (Can I Play That).
Other hardware (fingerprint or Windows Hello, cameras, speakers, headphone jack, haptics, gyro): Power button with fingerprint reader; two microphones above the left buttons; HD haptics with two motors per side; 6-axis gyro; RGB rings around the sticks. Impulse triggers are on the X model only (Wikipedia).

CONTROLS AND BUTTON LAYOUT
Xbox-controller-style grips. Left of screen: Command Center and View buttons, plus a larger Xbox button between them and the left stick. Right of screen: Library and Menu buttons. Full-size sticks, a flat round D-pad, face buttons sized like an Xbox pad with a bit more travel, light bumpers, very wide triggers, two remappable back buttons. Top edge: power/fingerprint, microSD, headphone jack, volume, two USB-C (Can I Play That).

HOW IT FEELS
Can I Play That describes it as like holding a wide Xbox controller: real handle grips that hang below the screen, a body thicker than a Switch 2, but comfortable because the handles spread the weight. All buttons are within comfortable reach. The D-pad is flatter and rounder than an Xbox pad's.

BLIND USABILITY
No keyboard is built in, so the standard Narrator shortcut (Windows key+Ctrl+Enter) needs a USB or Bluetooth keyboard. Microsoft's alternative for touch devices, Windows button+Volume Up, needs a hardware Windows button, which this device does not list in its controls, so it should not be relied on (UNCERTAIN whether the maker maps a combination to it). Can I Play That's review of the ROG Xbox Ally X found Narrator is available right away during Windows setup, but setup only mentions keyboard combinations, not controller buttons, and blind users may struggle to turn it on with the small on-screen icons. The same applies to any Windows 11 handheld. Plan to plug in a keyboard for first setup. Once Windows is set up, turn on Start Narrator before sign-in (or install NVDA), and remap a spare back button to a keyboard shortcut if the maker's software allows it. If Xbox mode (Microsoft's console-style full screen experience) is set to start at sign-in, Microsoft says other apps start only the first time you switch to the desktop. Set NVDA to Start at log in under Settings, Apps, Startup, or it may not be running in Xbox mode (Microsoft Support, KB5070297). Win+F11 enters and leaves Xbox mode. Armoury Crate SE: an AppleVis user with five months on an ROG Xbox Ally X said the Xbox full screen experience is fine to navigate with NVDA or Narrator, though not the best interface, and that Armoury Crate is "surprisingly accessible even down to remapping the embedded controller"; another AppleVis user returned theirs and found it frustrating, preferring Steam Big Picture. A blind Reddit user planning an Ally said he would connect a keyboard for initial setup. Can I Play That reports Armoury Crate can remap every button except the Xbox, Command Center and Library buttons, with macros of up to five key presses on one input, so a back button can be set to Windows+Ctrl+Enter style combinations where the macro allows (combination support per key not confirmed, UNCERTAIN).

GAME COMPATIBILITY
Performance tier: mid (roughly Steam Deck class, Zen 2 with 8 RDNA 2 units). Microsoft's Handheld Compatibility Program labels games as handheld optimized or mostly compatible; a Bazzite (Linux) analysis cited by Wikipedia found higher frame rates than Windows, but that removes NVDA. Audio games and accessible indies run fine.

UNCERTAIN
None beyond noted.

ENTRY: ROG Xbox Ally X
Maker: ASUS with Microsoft
Released: October 16, 2025 (Wikipedia).
Launch price: US$999.99; EUR 899 (Wikipedia; Can I Play That). Notebookcheck noted the MSI Claw A8 was EUR 100 more than the Xbox Ally X in Europe.

WHAT IT CAN DO
The high-end Xbox Ally with AMD's Ryzen AI Z2 Extreme, more memory and storage, impulse triggers and a bigger battery.

HARDWARE
Processor: AMD Ryzen AI Z2 Extreme: octa-core Zen 5 (mix of Zen 5 and Zen 5c per Notebookcheck's Claw A8 review of the same chip) with an NPU.
Memory: 24 GB LPDDR5X; soldered.
Graphics: AMD RDNA 3.5, 16 compute units at 2.7 GHz, about 5.5 TFLOPS (Radeon 890M class).
Storage: 1 TB M.2 SSD, replaceable; microSD.
Display: 7-inch IPS 1920 by 1080, 120 Hz, VRR.
Battery: 80 Wh.
Weight and size: 715 grams (Wikipedia); Can I Play That measured 721 grams.
Operating system: Windows 11 Home with Xbox mode.
Wi-Fi and network: Wi-Fi 6E 2x2.
Bluetooth: Bluetooth 5.4.
Cellular: None.
Ports and expansion (USB4, Thunderbolt, eGPU, video out, microSD, docking): As ROG Xbox Ally: two USB-C, microSD, 3.5 mm jack.
Other hardware (fingerprint or Windows Hello, cameras, speakers, headphone jack, haptics, gyro): Fingerprint power button, HD haptics, gyro, impulse triggers, NPU, Gaming Copilot beta.

CONTROLS AND BUTTON LAYOUT
As ROG Xbox Ally.

HOW IT FEELS
Same grip shape as the Xbox Ally, about 45 grams heavier. Can I Play That: easy to handle despite weight; the speakers get quite loud with very good stereo but somewhat flat bass, and only the screen and rear vents got warm.

BLIND USABILITY
