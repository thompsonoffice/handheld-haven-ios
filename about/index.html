Handheld Haven

Accessible handheld PC guide. For iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later. Coming to the App Store.

Handheld Haven is an accessible encyclopedia of handheld PCs, UMPCs, and small Windows computers, written for blind and low-vision buyers and users. All 75 device entries and the guides are built into the app, so it works offline. It is designed around VoiceOver on iPhone and iPad.

What'S Inside

  • 75 device entries in 11 chapters: the GPD Win series; GPD Pocket, MicroPC and Duo; the ASUS ROG Ally family; the Lenovo Legion Go family; the MSI Claw family; AYANEO; OneXPlayer and OneXFly; other handhelds and the Steam Deck; Microsoft Surface; Dell Venue and small Windows tablets; and UMPCs and Project Origami
  • A blind buyer's guide: should you buy a handheld, and what to avoid
  • Screen reader setup on handheld and small Windows devices
  • History and eras of handheld Windows computers
  • A glossary of 34 terms, with "See" references
  • The list of sources used for the book

Features

  • Browse by chapter, then by device
  • Device pages with headings, such as Hardware and Sources, so the VoiceOver headings rotor can move through an entry
  • Full-text search across devices, guides, and the glossary. Every word you type must match.
  • Compare two devices: side by side on iPad, and stacked under each fact heading on iPhone, with an option to show only the differences. When an entry does not list a fact, it says "Not stated in this entry."
  • Favorites and recently viewed entries, saved only on your device
  • Share an entry as a plain text file
  • A text size setting that goes up to the largest accessibility size, plus a high contrast option. The app follows Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion.

Private And Offline

No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. Handheld Haven collects no data. Source web addresses are shown as text, and the app does not open them.

Honest Notes

Handheld Haven was compiled with AI assistance from public sources, then structured for screen readers. Prices are launch prices from the dates shown, not current prices. "Not documented" means no source used for the book stated it, not that a feature is missing. Nobody involved in writing the book tested these devices with a screen reader; blind usability notes come from published reviews, forum reports from blind users, and maker documentation. Check anything important, especially prices and compatibility, before you buy.

Independent

Handheld Haven is an independent guide. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any manufacturer. Product names are used only to identify the devices, and there are no manufacturer logos or product images.
