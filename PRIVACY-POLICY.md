# Handheld Haven Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

App: Handheld Haven (iPhone and iPad). Developer: thompsonoffice.

## Summary

Handheld Haven does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information. The developer receives no data from the app.

## Works offline

All reference data is included in the app. Handheld Haven makes no network requests of its own and works with no internet connection. Source addresses from the book are shown as text. The app does not open them.

## No accounts, ads, or tracking

There are no accounts, sign-ins, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party software development kits.

## What stays on your device

Your favorites, recently viewed entries, comparison picks, text size, and high contrast setting are saved only on your own device, in the app's local settings. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them. They may be included in your own device backups, which Apple handles under its privacy policy.

## Sharing

When you choose Share as TXT, the text goes only where you send it.

## Children

Handheld Haven collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear in the app and on its web page with a new effective date.

## App Store privacy label

Data Not Collected.
